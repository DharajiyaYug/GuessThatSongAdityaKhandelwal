1 · Connect your Spotify

Connecting lets you import your own playlists and Liked Songs, and save songs from the game to your Liked Songs or a playlist. Songs you import here are kept in this browser and mixed into your game.

Spotify says “not registered” / you can't connect?

Spotify only lets a small number of people use a hobby app. Either ask the site owner to add your Spotify email, or use your own free Spotify developer app (needs Spotify Premium):

  1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard → Create app.
  2. Redirect URI: · tick Web API · Save.
  3. Copy the Client ID, paste it below, then click Connect Spotify.

2 · Import a Spotify playlist

Spotify only lets apps read playlists you own or collaborate on. For anyone else's playlist: open it in the Spotify app → ⋯ → Add to other playlist → New playlist, then import your copy.
