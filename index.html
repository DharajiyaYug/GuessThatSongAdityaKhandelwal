How to play

Pick Hindi, English or both, any eras and any genres. Press PLAY to hear a recognisable line of the song — each round picks one at random (often the chorus), starting with just 0.1 seconds. Each SKIP or wrong guess unlocks a longer clip: 0.1 s → 0.2 s → 0.5 s → 1 s → 3 s → 8 s → 15 s. Faster correct guesses earn more points.

Type the song title, the movie, the singer, or a line of the lyrics and pick from the suggestions. Songs are random and never repeat until you reset your played history. Choose a mode: Easy gives you 5 strikes, Medium 3, and Hard none — the first missed song ends the game. A strike is a song you don't get (or give up on). Each mode has its own 🏆 leaderboard for best game score and best streak. Connect Spotify to save songs you like straight to your Liked Songs or a playlist, and to mix your own playlists into the game.

Keys: Space play · ↓↑ + Enter pick a suggestion · Shift+S skip · N next song.

Audio plays through the official Spotify player — stay logged in to Spotify in this browser for full songs (otherwise Spotify only allows 30-second previews).

Settings

Sound

The songs play through Spotify at your device's volume — use your computer's or phone's volume keys (Spotify's player doesn't let websites change its loudness).

Search

Turning it off makes the page lighter (lyrics aren't downloaded) and suggestions only match title, movie and artist.

Spotify

You don't need to connect to play. Connecting lets you save songs to your Liked Songs or a playlist, import your playlists, and puts your Spotify name on the leaderboard.

Spotify says “not registered” or “user not allowed”? Use your own Spotify app (free, ~3 minutes)

Spotify lets each hobby app connect only 5 people, so the site's own app is full. You can make your own app instead; it only needs to work for you. You need Spotify Premium, and Spotify allows one such app per person (if you already have one, just add the address from step 4 to it).

  1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your Spotify account. Accept the developer terms if asked.
  2. Click Create app.
  3. App name: anything, e.g. My Song Game. App description: anything, e.g. Personal song guessing game. Website: leave empty.
  4. Redirect URI: paste and click Add.
  5. Under “Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?”, tick Web API. Tick the terms box and click Save.
  6. On your app's page, copy the Client ID (a 32-character code — not the Client secret).
  7. Paste it here and click Save & connect:

Your Client ID is stored only in this browser. You'll see Spotify's permission screen once; after that you're connected under your own name. The Client ID is not a password, but never paste your Client secret anywhere.
